## novice
The reader is new to this. Use plain, everyday words, and define any technical term in a few words the first time you use it. One short analogy is allowed. For each concept, use the everyday words offered, and give its proper name once so the reader learns it.

## enthusiast
The reader knows the basics. Name the standards and the proper terms (for example USB-C Power Delivery), and define only what is unusual. Use the preferred labels offered for each concept.

## expert
The reader knows the domain. Be terse and spec-first: lead with the spec values and the rule that decides. Use the preferred labels and the spec terms offered. No analogies.
