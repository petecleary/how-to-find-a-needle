AI does not replace good search, data structures or information architecture. **It makes them more important.**

1. **Structure first.** Filters are exact, and only as good as the attributes you store.
2. **Words and meaning are both evidence.** Keyword search misses synonyms, and vector search misses fit. [Hybrid search](term:hybrid-search) keeps both.
3. **Similar is not compatible.** A small [ontology](term:ontology) of names and rules turns a near miss into a flagged result, with its reason.
4. **An LLM explains what search found.** It is only as good as the evidence it is given.

The code, the data, the decisions and this talk are one repository. Every trace you saw is there to read.
